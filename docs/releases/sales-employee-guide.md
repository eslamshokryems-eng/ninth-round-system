# Sales — Employee & Manager Guide

This guide is for sales employees and branch managers. It assumes no technical knowledge.

## Signing in
* Always use the official address **app.9throundegypt.com**. Do not use links ending in `vercel.app`.
* On a new phone or computer, the system asks for a **6-digit code**. The code is sent to your administrator, so ask them for it. Tick "Trust this device" so you are not asked again on that device for 30 days.
* If the page is slow or your connection drops, you stay signed in. Wait a moment, or refresh the page.

## Sales employee — your day
Open **Sales**. You land on **My Sales Workspace**, a list of only *your* leads, in the order you should work them:

1. **Overdue**: the agreed time has passed. Do these first.
2. **Due today**.
3. **New, never contacted**: make the first contact today.
4. **Ready to convert**: the customer said they are ready to join.
5. **Hot** leads.
6. **Awaiting a reply**: you sent a message.
7. **No next action**: schedule one.
8. **Scheduled**: nothing due yet.

Each card says *why* the lead is there (for example "Overdue by 2 day(s)"). It also shows the phone, source, status, priority (hot / warm / cold), the last contact, and the next action with its date.

**Buttons on each lead**
* **Call**: dials the number on a phone.
* **Open WhatsApp**: opens the chat. The system never sends messages for you.
* **Record contact**: say what you did (call / WhatsApp / visit) and how it went. You must also choose the **next action and its date**. You cannot save without it, because every active lead must have a next step. If the customer is not interested, choose "Not interested (close the lead)" and pick a reason.
* **Schedule follow-up**: set or move the next action.
* **Add note**.
* **Mark as hot / warm / cold**.
* **Open lead**: the full page with the complete history.

**Follow-ups page** (Sales → Follow-ups): lists your follow-ups due today and overdue.
* **Record contact** completes the follow-up and schedules the next one.
* **Reschedule** moves it to a new date.

**Lead page**
* **History**: every call, message, visit, note, status change, owner change and conversion, with the employee's name and time. History cannot be edited or deleted.
* **Mark as Lost**: choose a reason. The lead is closed and its follow-up is cancelled.
* **Reopen lead** (on a lost lead): choose the next action and its date.
* **Convert to Member**: see below.

## Converting a lead to a member
1. Open the lead and press **Convert to Member**.
2. The form shows **Sold by (credited)**. This is always the **lead owner**. You are recorded as the employee who registered the membership and received the payment.
3. Choose the membership. The price is filled in; change it if needed. Then enter:
   * the receipt number;
   * the start date;
   * the payment method;
   * optionally the discount, program, date of birth, national ID and notes.
4. Press **Confirm Conversion** once. The member, membership and payment are created together, and the lead becomes *Converted*.

If something is wrong, nothing is created and a message tells you what to fix:
* "Receipt number … is already used".
* "This phone number already belongs to a member". In that case, renew or add a membership from the member's profile instead.
* "Assign this lead … before converting" (a manager must assign it first).
* "The lead owner is no longer active" (a manager must reassign it).

Pressing the button twice, or retrying after a connection problem, **never** creates a second membership. The system shows the existing one.

## Branch manager
* **Sales → Leads**: every lead of your branch, including **unassigned** leads. Unassigned leads are visible only to managers.
* **Assign / reassign** from a lead page: choose the employee and **write the reason** (it is required). Every change is recorded with who, when and why.
* **Sales → Team dashboard** shows:
  * active, unassigned, overdue, hot, no-next-action and no-recent-contact leads;
  * contacts recorded;
  * converted and lost;
  * conversion rates, with their definition shown next to them;
  * a table per employee, with a "needs support" flag where a workload looks unhealthy;
  * lists of neglected leads, with filters, bulk assign/reassign, and each employee's worklist.
* You can convert any lead of your branch. The **owner** stays credited as Sold By.
* You cannot see or change another branch's leads.

## What is not possible (by design)
* Employees cannot see other employees' leads or the unassigned inbox, and cannot give a lead to someone else.
* Nobody can mark a lead converted without registering its membership.
* Nobody can complete a follow-up without saying what happened and what comes next.
* Nobody can edit or delete history.

## دليل مختصر بالعربي

**الدخول:** استخدم دايمًا **app.9throundegypt.com**. لو دخلت من جهاز جديد هيطلب منك كود من 6 أرقام، والكود ده بيروح للمسؤول بتاعك، فاطلبه منه.

**يومك كموظف مبيعات:** افتح **Sales**، هتلاقي قائمة فيها عملاءك أنت بس، ومرتبة حسب الأولوية: المتأخر الأول، بعده المطلوب النهارده، وبعده العملاء الجداد اللي لسه ما اتكلمتش معاهم.

**تسجيل تواصل:** اختار عملت إيه (مكالمة / واتساب / زيارة) والنتيجة كانت إيه. **لازم** تحدد الخطوة الجاية وميعادها، ومش هيتحفظ من غيرها. لو العميل مش مهتم، اختار "Not interested" واكتب السبب.

**التحويل لعضو:** من صفحة العميل دوس "Convert to Member" واختار الاشتراك. اكتب رقم الإيصال وطريقة الدفع وأكد. البيعة **بتتحسب دايمًا لصاحب العميل**، وأنت بتتسجل على إنك اللي عملت الاشتراك واستلمت الفلوس. لو دوست مرتين أو النت قطع وعدت المحاولة، **مش هيتعمل اشتراك تاني**.

**المدير:** بيشوف كل عملاء الفرع، ومنهم العملاء اللي مش متوزعين على حد. بيوزع العملاء أو بينقلهم لموظف تاني، ولازم يكتب السبب. لوحة الفريق بتوضح مين محتاج دعم، والعملاء المهملين، ونسبة التحويل وطريقة حسابها.
